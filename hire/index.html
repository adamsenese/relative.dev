Good questions04
We're not very technical. Is that a problem?+

Not at all. That's who we built this for. If your team can write an email, they can use what we set up. We handle the technical side and teach everything in plain language.

What does Claude cost on top of this?+

Claude plans are billed by Anthropic, not by us. For most teams it's a modest monthly amount per person, and we'll recommend the right plan during the audit so you see the full picture before you commit.

Is our client data safe?+

Every workflow keeps a person reviewing before anything goes out. We set up business-grade Claude plans where your data isn't used for model training, and we keep sensitive records where they already live. If you're in a regulated field, we work within your compliance requirements.

What happens when the package ends?+

Everything we build is yours, and it's documented. Your support window covers questions. After that, the optional Care Plan ($99/mo, billed annually) keeps hosting, updates, and basic support in good hands. There's no lock-in.
