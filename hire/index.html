    ◆Good questions

    Things people usually ask.

    What's actually in the free plan?+

    A short, plain-English document written for your business: 10 specific ways to save time or win more customers, the 3 we'd do first (all doable within a week), and a rough estimate of the hours each could give back. No strings attached.

    We're not very technical. Is that a problem?+

    Not at all. That's who we built this for. If your team can write an email, they can use what we set up. We handle the technical side and teach everything in plain language.

    What does Claude cost on top of your package?+

    Claude plans are billed by Anthropic, not by us, and for most teams the cost is a small monthly amount per person. We'll recommend the right plan for your size during the audit, so you'll know the full picture before you commit.

    Is our client data safe?+

    We design every workflow with a person reviewing before anything goes out, set up business-grade Claude plans where your data isn't used for model training, and keep sensitive records where they already live. If you're in a regulated field, we'll work within your compliance requirements.

    What happens after the package ends?+

    Everything we build is yours, and it's documented. Your follow-up support window covers questions. After that, the optional Care Plan ($99/mo, billed annually) covers hosting, updates, and basic support. If you want bigger projects later, we're happy to scope them. There's no lock-in.
